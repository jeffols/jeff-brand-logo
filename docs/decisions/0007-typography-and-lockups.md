# 0007 — Typography and lockups

**Date:** 2026-08-01
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Three registers, two of which ship to the web.

| Register | Face | Ships |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark and lockups | **IBM Plex Sans SemiBold**, converted to outlines | No — artwork |
| UI, navigation, headings | **Inter** | Yes |
| Essays and long-form | **iA Writer Duo S** | Yes |

Two lockup constructions, and only two:

- `horizontal` — mark beside **Jeff Olsen**
- `stacked` — mark above **jeffols**

Generated by `scripts/generate_lockups.py` into `assets/lockups/`.

## The j-collision

The mark is a lowercase **j**. The compact brand **jeffols** begins with one. Set
side by side they read as *"j jeffols"* — the mark stops being a symbol and
becomes a letter in the word.

The capital **J** in the full name breaks that echo. So:

- Horizontal lockups use **Jeff Olsen**.
- **jeffols** only ever stacks, or stands alone as text with no mark beside it.
- Never set the mark horizontally beside lowercase `jeffols`.

Confirmed against specimens in all four candidate typefaces. The collision is
present in every one; it is a property of the letterforms, not of the face.

## Why these three

**Plex Sans for the wordmark** — chosen for its capital J, which is the glyph the
horizontal lockup rests on.

**iA Writer Duo S for essays** — a duospace: mostly monospaced, selectively
widened for prose. Its licence carries two copyright lines, `iA Writer` and
`Plex`, because it is IBM Plex Mono rewidened. So the wordmark and the reading
face are one lineage rather than two unrelated choices.

**Inter for UI** — measurably better than Plex at 12–14px in dense interface
text, and the only one of the three with no Reserved Font Name, so it can be
subset freely.

Rejected: JetBrains Mono as the primary. Distinctive, but it typecasts as a
developer portfolio rather than the systems-thinker position the brand is built
on, and `Jeff Olsen` sprawls badly at wordmark sizes.

## Outlines, not webfonts

The lockup wordmark is converted to SVG paths. A lockup is therefore artwork, not
font software: it renders identically with no webfont loaded and no font
installed, and Plex Sans never reaches a browser. It is a design-time dependency,
kept at `assets/fonts/design-time/` so lockups stay regenerable.

This also means the site loads exactly two faces, the same as if the wordmark had
been set in Inter.

## Licensing constraints

All three are SIL OFL. Two carry **Reserved Font Names**:

| Face | Reserved name | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Inter | none | subset freely, keep the name |
| IBM Plex Sans | `Plex` | design-time only, so no runtime concern |
| iA Writer Duo S | `iA Writer`, `Plex` | **a subset may not keep the name** |

OFL clause 3 forbids a Modified Version from using a Reserved Font Name, and
subsetting produces a Modified Version. The site must either serve iA Writer Duo
unmodified, or subset it and rename the family internally. Serving unmodified is
simpler and is the recommended path.

Embedding outlined glyphs in artwork is not distributing font software, which is
the normal reading and the basis of the lockup approach. Not legal advice; the
caveat in `docs/legal-and-protection-notes.md` applies.

## `currentColor` needs inlining

`lockup-*-mono.svg` and `assets/marks/*/mark-mono.svg` use `fill="currentColor"`.
That inherits only when the SVG is **inlined into the HTML**. Referenced through
`<img src="...">` the SVG is an isolated document with no parent colour, and the
mark renders black.

Use the mono variants inline. Use a palette variant for `<img>`.

## Consequences

- `assets/fonts/` holds Inter and iA Writer Duo with both licence texts, plus
  Plex Sans under `design-time/`.
- `assets/lockups/` holds 10 files: two constructions × (mono, plus dark/light in
  plated and transparent forms).
- Lockups exist only for `signal_yellow` so far. Regenerate per palette with
  `--palette` if another is ever needed.
- Closes the last open item in BRAND.md section 20 before website work, and one
  of the "still to decide" items in section 23.

## Not settled here

Type scale, line length, and heading hierarchy. Those are site implementation
decisions and belong in the website repository, constrained by this record rather
than replacing it.
