Palette audit — distinctiveness and differentiation

Can two palettes be told apart, and does that survive colour blindness? Marks are shown as a viewer with each condition would see them. Separation is CIEDE2000 on the dark-mode glyph, where the differentiation actually lives — the plates are near-black and carry almost none of it.

ΔE under 10 is a collision: two palettes that read as the same colour. 10–20 is close. 20+ is unambiguously different.
